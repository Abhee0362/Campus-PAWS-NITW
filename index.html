Dog medical emergency? Let’s act quickly.

If you see a campus dog injured, bleeding, struggling to breathe, unable to stand, or in immediate distress, contact us so we can help coordinate support. Please share the exact campus location and a photo/video only if it is safe to take one.

🚨 Contact the Campus Paws team

Call or WhatsApp us with the location, what happened, and whether the dog is conscious and breathing.

Phone: +91 63038 24715

Request emergency help on WhatsApp

🩹 While help is on the way

  • Keep yourself and others at a safe distance; an injured or frightened dog may bite.
  • Do not force food or water, and do not give human medicines.
  • Do not move the dog unless staying there creates immediate danger.
  • If there is bleeding and it is safe to approach, use clean gauze or cloth with gentle, steady pressure. Avoid touching wounds directly.
  • Keep traffic and crowds away, and contact a veterinarian or local animal rescue service as soon as possible.

🎒 First-aid support

Campus Paws may help coordinate basic first-aid supplies and volunteer support when available. Availability is not guaranteed, and first aid is not a substitute for veterinary care.
